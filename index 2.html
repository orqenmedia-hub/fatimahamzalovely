from Hamza, with all my love 💗

Turn the box with your finger, then open it.

My dearest Fatima,

Do you remember the first time I saw you? We were playing tennikoit. It was only a game, but I stopped seeing the ring and the court, and I only saw you.

That same day I told all my friends, “I can’t get over her.” They laughed at me. I didn’t, because I meant every single word.

After that, you stayed in my dreams for so long. Every quiet moment, every night, my thoughts found their way back to you, and I kept it all to myself.

And now, finally, I mean something to you. I still can’t believe it is real. It is the best thing that has ever happened to me.

So I made you this little gift, with everything I never knew how to say out loud.

Fatima, I love you.

Forever yours,
Hamza

Tap the seal. Then there is one last thing…

One last thing, Fatima…

Will you marry Hamza?

She said yes!

Congratulations!
